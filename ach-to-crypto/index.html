ACH → Crypto

Convert your US dollars to cryptocurrency instantly using ACH (Automated Clearing House) transfers. Global Payment Solutions provides a seamless on-ramp solution for individuals and businesses looking to enter the crypto market.

What is ACH to Crypto?

ACH to Crypto is a payment method that allows you to transfer funds from your US bank account directly to purchase cryptocurrency. This service bridges traditional banking with the digital asset ecosystem, making it easy to convert fiat currency into stablecoins like USDC, USDT, or other supported cryptocurrencies.

How It Works

1

Link Your Bank Account

Connect your US bank account to Global Payment Solutions securely using our encrypted platform.

2

Initiate Transfer

Specify the amount you want to convert and select your desired cryptocurrency.

3

Receive Crypto

Your cryptocurrency is delivered to by Bridge within 1-3 business days. Global Payment Solutions does not hold the assets.

Key Benefits

  • Low Fees: A new customer pays a 1% conversion total: 0.9% Global Payment Solutions plus 0.1% Bridge. An agreed personal rate stays as agreed.
  • Bank-Level Security: Global Payment Solutions does not hold the funds. Bridge converts and holds the assets under Bridge's terms.
  • High Limits: Transfer up to $25,000 per transaction for verified accounts
  • Reversals: A bank can still reverse an ACH payment. Global Payment Solutions does not promise that it is final

Processing Time

Standard ACH transfers typically take 1-3 business days to complete. For faster processing, consider using ACH Same Day or Wire transfers (additional fees may apply).
