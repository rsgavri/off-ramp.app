Crypto ↔ GBP

Move money between British pounds and cryptocurrency using UK Faster Payments — the instant payment rail connecting every major UK bank. Global Payment Solutions gives you both directions: receive crypto by sending GBP from your bank, and cash out crypto straight to your UK account.

What is the GBP corridor?

Faster Payments is the UK's 24/7 instant bank-transfer network. Global Payment Solutions connects it to the crypto world through regulated banking partners: you get personal GBP account details in your own name, and transfers convert automatically between GBP and stablecoins at the live mid-market rate with one flat 1% fee.

GBP → Crypto: receive crypto via UK bank transfer

1

Create your GBP virtual account

In the app, choose the England corridor, pick the coin and network you want to receive (USDT on Tron (TRC-20) or USDC on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum or Solana) and the wallet address. You instantly get UK account details — sort code and account number — issued in your name.

2

Send GBP from any UK bank

Make a normal Faster Payments transfer to your virtual account details. Most transfers arrive within minutes, any time of day.

3

Crypto arrives automatically

Every deposit converts automatically and lands at your chosen wallet address. The account is permanent — all future GBP deposits keep converting with no extra steps.

Crypto → GBP: cash out to your UK bank

1

Add your UK bank account

Enter the account holder name and bank details (IBAN and BIC) once — they are saved for future withdrawals.

2

Get your personal USDC deposit address

Choose the network you will send from — Base (lowest fees), Ethereum (most compatible) or Solana (fast) — and we generate a deposit address linked to your bank account.

3

Send USDC and receive GBP

Funds are converted and paid out to your account via Faster Payments, typically within 1–3 business days. The address is reusable: every future USDC transfer to it pays out to the same bank account.
