Convert your cryptocurrency to fiat currency and receive funds via international wire transfer to any bank account worldwide. Global Payment Solutions provides fast, secure global money transfers with competitive exchange rates.

What is Crypto to WIRE?

Crypto to WIRE is a premium off-ramp service that allows you to sell your cryptocurrency and receive funds via international wire transfer (SWIFT) to any bank account globally. Wire transfers are ideal for large transactions and international payments where speed and reliability are essential.

How It Works

1

Add Bank Details

Provide your international bank account details including SWIFT/BIC code and account number.

2

Sell Cryptocurrency

Select the crypto you want to sell and specify the fiat amount and currency (USD, EUR, GBP, etc.).

3

Receive Funds

Your funds arrive in your bank account within 1-3 business days via international wire transfer.
