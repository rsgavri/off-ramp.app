Cookie Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

At Global Payment Solutions, we use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze site traffic, and understand where our visitors are coming from.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility.
  • Analytics Cookies: After you press Accept, the site may load a Google advertising tag (AW-17779747081) and Google Analytics (G-SQ73WY8WMV). They are not required for the service. Decline means those tags are not loaded. The choice is stored in the cookie global payment solutions_cookie_consent for 365 days.
  • Preference Cookies: These cookies remember your preferences, such as dark mode settings and language preferences.
  • Security Cookies: Account creation uses FingerprintJS to detect fraud. That tool is a security measure for registration, not an advertising cookie. The rest of the public site does not load it.

Types of Cookies We Use

Session Cookies

These are temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser. They help us maintain your session while you navigate through our website.

Persistent Cookies

These cookies remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them. They help us remember your preferences and provide a personalized experience.

Third-Party Cookies

If you accept, Google may set cookies for the advertising tag and Analytics. Essential cookies and the registration security check do not wait for that choice.

Managing Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by clicking on the cookie settings in our cookie banner when you first visit our website.

You can also set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies. If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our website, though your access to some functionality and areas may be restricted.

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. However, if you limit the ability of websites to set cookies, you may worsen your overall user experience.

Third-Party Services

  • Google tags, only after Accept: advertising tag AW-17779747081 and Analytics G-SQ73WY8WMV
  • FingerprintJS: used only on account creation, to detect fraud.
