What is Crypto to SEPA?

Crypto to SEPA is an off-ramp service that allows you to sell your cryptocurrency and receive euros in your European bank account. SEPA is the payment integration initiative of the European Union for simplification of bank transfers denominated in euros, covering all 27 EU member states plus 9 additional countries.
