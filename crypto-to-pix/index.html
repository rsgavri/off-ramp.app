Crypto ↔ BRL (PIX)

Move money between Brazilian reals and cryptocurrency using PIX — Brazil's instant payment system that works 24/7 and settles in seconds. Receive crypto by paying a PIX QR code, cash out crypto to any PIX key, or pay any PIX charge directly from your USDC balance.

What is PIX?

PIX is the instant payment system created by the Central Bank of Brazil. Transfers settle in seconds, around the clock, every day of the year. Global Payment Solutions (3-102-151245 S.R.L., Cédula Jurídica 3-102-151245) connects PIX to the crypto world: deposits convert automatically into stablecoins, and crypto converts back into BRL paid to any PIX key — always at the live mid-market rate Bridge converts and holds the funds. A new customer without a personal rate pays a 1% conversion total (0.9% Global Payment Solutions plus 0.1% Bridge).

BRL → Crypto: receive crypto via PIX

1

Create your PIX virtual account

Choose the Brazil corridor, pick the coin and network to receive (USDT on Tron (TRC-20) or USDC on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum or Solana) and your wallet address. You get a personal PIX QR code (copia e cola).

2

Pay the QR from any Brazilian bank app

Scan or paste the code in your banking app and confirm. PIX transfers complete in seconds, 24/7.

3

Crypto arrives automatically

The deposit converts automatically and lands at your wallet address. The QR code is permanent — every future PIX payment to it converts the same way.

Crypto → BRL: cash out to any PIX key

1

Add your PIX key

CPF, phone number (+55…), email or a random key (chave aleatória) — entered once and saved for future withdrawals.

2

Get your personal USDC deposit address

Choose Base, Ethereum or Solana, and we generate a deposit address linked to your PIX key.

3

Send USDC and receive BRL

Funds convert and are paid out via PIX — typically the same day. The address is reusable for all future cash-outs to the same key.

PIX Pay: spend USDC anywhere PIX is accepted

In the Brazil corridor you will find PIX Pay — paste any PIX QR code or enter any PIX key, see the exact BRL amount and the USDC that will be debited, and confirm. The recipient gets BRL instantly. Live in Brazil on a crypto balance — no Brazilian bank account needed.
