Privacy requests are answered at that e-mail address.

Data we use

Identity and contact data, including name, e-mail, phone, address, date of birth, tax and identity-document data, and, for a company, constitutional documents and beneficial owners. Financial and transaction data, including payment references, amounts, wallets and the status of a transfer. Device and security data from the website and the mobile application: a first-party installation identifier, application version, operating system, IP address, approximate country, login time and application activity. The Company does not collect an advertising identifier or precise GPS location for that purpose. Support messages the User sends us.

Why we use it

  • To open and run the account and to perform the service the User asks for (contractual performance and informed consent under Law No. 8968).
  • To meet legal duties, including identity, sanctions and anti-money-laundering duties under Costa Rican Law No. 7786 / Law No. 8204 and international sanctions obligations.
  • To prevent fraud, protect the account and keep records of the User's acceptance of these policies.
  • To answer support requests and to send a service message.
  • Marketing or an advertising cookie only where the User has accepted it. The User can refuse it without losing the service.

Who receives it

The Company shares personal data with the Partner Provider the User chooses, so that provider can perform the regulated service and apply its own terms: Bridge Ventures Inc.; 1Money USA, Inc.; Noah US, Inc. and Lead Bank; and Attrus and FacilitaPay North America LLC and its sponsor bank. Each of those providers handles the account it opens as its own controller. The Company also uses infrastructure, e-mail and, only after cookie consent, Google's advertising tag. The Company may disclose data to a competent authority when the law requires it. The Company does not sell personal data.

International transfers

Partner Providers and some of their banks are in the United States, the European Union or other jurisdictions outside Costa Rica. The transfer is made because the User asked for that service, or because an anti-money-laundering or sanctions duty requires it, in accordance with Costa Rican Law No. 8968. It is limited to the data needed for the stated purpose.

How long we keep it

Account, identity, transaction, fraud and sanctions records are kept for at least five years after the account closes or after the transaction, and longer while a legal claim, an investigation or a tax duty is open, in accordance with Costa Rican Law No. 7786 / Law No. 8204 and applicable anti-money-laundering retention standards. Other personal data is deleted or anonymized when it is no longer needed. Details of account deletion are on the account-deletion page.

The User's rights

Under Costa Rican Law No. 8968 (and where applicable GDPR/other local data-protection laws), the User may ask for confirmation, access, rectification, update or deletion of personal data, information about who received the data, and revocation of consent. A complaint may be made to the Costa Rican data-protection authority (Agencia de Protección de Datos de los Habitantes — PRODhab) or the competent authority in the User's jurisdiction. Some records cannot be deleted before the statutory retention period ends. The Company will explain when that applies.

Children

The service is only for people aged 18 or over. The Company does not knowingly collect personal data from a child.

Security and incidents

The Company uses access controls and encryption appropriate to the service. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. A security incident that may create relevant risk is handled in accordance with Costa Rican Law No. 8968 and its regulations, including notice to the competent authority and affected Users where required by law.

Cookies

Essential cookies, including the cookie that stores the User's accept or decline choice, are used without separate consent because the site needs them. The Google advertising tag is not essential and loads only after the User chooses Accept. Details are in the Cookie Policy.

Changes and contact

The Company may update this policy. A material change is posted on this page before it applies. Questions and requests: ceo@off-ramp.app. These rules follow the laws of the Republic of Costa Rica.
