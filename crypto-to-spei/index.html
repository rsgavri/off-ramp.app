Crypto ↔ MXN (SPEI)

Move money between Mexican pesos and cryptocurrency using SPEI — Mexico's interbank electronic transfer system operated by Banco de México, available 24/7/365. Receive crypto by sending pesos to your personal CLABE, and cash out crypto to any Mexican bank account.

What is SPEI?

SPEI (Sistema de Pagos Electrónicos Interbancarios) is Mexico's national real-time payment network — transfers between Mexican banks settle in seconds at any hour. Global Payment Solutions connects SPEI to the crypto world: you get a personal 18-digit CLABE in your name, and transfers convert automatically between MXN and stablecoins at the live mid-market rate Bridge converts and holds the funds. A new customer without a personal rate pays a 1% conversion total (0.9% Global Payment Solutions plus 0.1% Bridge).

MXN → Crypto: receive crypto via SPEI

1

Create your MXN virtual account

Choose the Mexico corridor, pick the coin and network to receive (USDT on Tron (TRC-20) or USDC on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum or Solana) and your wallet address. You get a personal CLABE — the standard 18-digit Mexican bank account number.

2

Send pesos from any Mexican bank

Make a normal SPEI transfer to your CLABE from any bank or fintech app. SPEI runs 24/7 and usually settles within seconds.

3

Crypto arrives automatically

The deposit converts automatically and lands at your wallet address. The CLABE is permanent — every future SPEI deposit keeps converting with no extra steps.

Crypto → MXN: cash out to any Mexican bank

1

Add your CLABE

Enter the account holder name and the 18-digit CLABE of the destination account — saved for future withdrawals.

2

Get your personal USDC deposit address

Choose Base, Ethereum or Solana, and we generate a deposit address linked to your CLABE.

3

Send USDC and receive MXN

Funds convert and are paid out via SPEI — typically the same day. The address is reusable for all future cash-outs to the same account.
