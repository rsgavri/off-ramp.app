Local corridors. Bridge converts.

3-102-151245 S.R.L. (EX.financial) provides this screen. Bridge converts between a local rail — PIX, SEPA, Faster Payments, SPEI or ACH — and stablecoins, where Bridge can serve the customer. PIX — Brazil

BRL ↔ Crypto
Instant · 24/7

Brazil's instant payment rail. Where Bridge can serve the customer, Bridge converts a PIX payment and holds the funds. EX.financial does not hold the assets.

  • Permanent personal QR code (copia e cola) — every deposit converts automatically
  • Cash out to any PIX key: CPF, phone, email or chave aleatória
  • PIX Pay: pay any PIX QR code or key straight from your USDC balance
  • EX.financial adds no separate PIX transfer fee. Timing follows PIX and Bridge.

SEPA — Europe

EUR ↔ Crypto
36 Countries

Where Bridge can serve the customer, Bridge converts between euros and stablecoins on SEPA. EX.financial does not hold the funds or the account.

SWIFT Wire — Global

Crypto → Any Bank Worldwide
Up to $250,000

Where Bridge can send a wire, Bridge converts and pays the beneficiary bank. EX.financial does not hold the funds and does not promise every bank or currency.
