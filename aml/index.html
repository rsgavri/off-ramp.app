Core control framework

The full manuals establish ownership, evidence requirements, escalation paths and review cycles across the customer and transaction lifecycle.

01

Governance & accountability

Board oversight, a designated MLRO, three lines of defence, management reporting, change control and annual review.

02

KYC, KYB & UBO

Identity and business verification, beneficial ownership, purpose of account, source of funds and enhanced due diligence.

03

Risk classification

Customer, geography, product, channel and delivery risks inform onboarding decisions, limits and review frequency.

04

Sanctions & PEP screening

Screening at onboarding and on an ongoing basis, with documented match resolution, escalation and no-tipping-off controls.

05

Transaction monitoring

Defined scenarios for structuring, velocity, rapid pass-through, profile deviation, third-party funding and corridor risk.

06

Virtual-asset controls

Wallet ownership checks, blockchain exposure review, Travel Rule readiness and restrictions for mixers and illicit services.

07

Case management

Alert triage, investigation records, disposition standards, escalation, suspicious-activity reporting and confidentiality.

08

Records & privacy

Controlled evidence retention, access security, data minimisation and alignment with Costa Rican Law No. 8968 obligations.

09

Assurance & training

Role-based training, quality assurance, provider oversight, independent testing and remediation tracking.

Risk-based, not threshold-only

No single transaction value determines risk. Decisions consider the customer profile, expected activity, counterparties, geographies, source and destination of funds, wallet exposure, behavioural patterns and available evidence together.

Implementation status

Publication of a policy is one component of an AML program. Formal ownership, systems, operating evidence and independent testing remain necessary before effectiveness can be asserted.

Reference framework

The program uses a layered approach: applicable Costa Rican law first, supplemented by FATF principles and partner requirements where they are relevant.

Costa Rican Law No. 7786 / Law No. 8204 (AML/CFT) ↗ Costa Rican Law No. 8968 (Data Protection) ↗ Registro Nacional de Costa Rica (Cédula Jurídica 3-102-151245) ↗ FATF Recommendations ↗
